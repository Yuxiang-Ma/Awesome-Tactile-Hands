# Datasets whose terms forbid redistribution

Samples of these datasets are not hosted in the viewer. Each row quotes the wording, so permission can be requested.

| dataset | wording | where |
|---|---|---|
| STAG (`stag`, Sundaram et al., Nature 2019, MIT CSAIL) | "The Licensed Material will not be copied nor distributed in any form other than for Your backup." (License Agreement for Use of Scalable Tactile Glove (STAG) Data and Models, research use only, non-commercial; Licensed Material covers the data, models and related source) | http://stag.csail.mit.edu/license.html (identical in https://github.com/Erkil1452/touch/blob/master/LICENSE) |
| ACE-Data-0 (`ace_data_0`, Cao et al., arXiv 2607.28625, NTU S-Lab + ACE Robotics) | "3.2 Distribute, publish, transmit, sublicense, sell, rent, lend, host, mirror, or otherwise make the Dataset available, in whole or in part, to any person who has not been granted access individually and directly by the Licensors. This includes shared drives, public or private code repositories, torrents, model hubs, and inclusion in any other dataset or collection." (ACE-Data-0 Research License Agreement v1.0, non-commercial academic research only) | https://huggingface.co/datasets/ACERobotics/ACE-Data-0/blob/main/LICENSE |
