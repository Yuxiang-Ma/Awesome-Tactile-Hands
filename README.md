# Awesome Tactile Hands [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> **Taxel Atlas**: a curated catalog of public datasets that record touch across the hand during manipulation, from human tactile gloves to dexterous robot hands with tactile skins.

Browse, filter and compare them on the [live catalog](https://yuxiang-ma.github.io/Awesome-Tactile-Hands/), and play recorded samples in the [data viewer](https://yuxiang-ma.github.io/Awesome-Tactile-Hands/viewer/).

**What gets in.** Tactile sensing *on the hand* (glove or robot hand), recorded while the hand manipulates objects, with data that is released or announced. Each entry states which regions are sensed (fingertips, finger segments, palm), how many taxels per hand, what else is recorded in sync, and how to get it. Numbers are as the authors report them; anything we could not confirm from a primary source is marked as not stated.

**What stays out.** Contact estimated from cameras, pressure pads under the hand, or thermal contact maps on objects (PressureVision, EgoPressure, ContactPose, ContactDB); parallel-jaw grippers; tactile hardware papers with no data.

## Human tactile gloves

- [EgoTactile](https://egotactile.github.io/) - Full-hand 162-sensor pressure glove with egocentric video of grasps on 63 everyday objects, plus a bare-hand test set. 162 taxels/hand; fingertips, finger segments, palm; 5.82 h; cameras: egocentric; hand tracking: none; public. ICML 2026 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09243), [project](https://egotactile.github.io/), [data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HustleHard/EgoTactile), [code](https://github.com/Russell-Zeng/EgoTactile)).
- [EgoTouch](https://jianyi2004.github.io/TouchAnything-Website/) - Bimanual pressure gloves with Rokoko finger tracking and Vive wrist trackers, a chest camera and two fisheye wrist cameras, 208 tasks in 5 kinds of scenes. 123 taxels/hand; fingertips, finger segments, palm; 20 h, 208 tasks, 5 environments; cameras: egocentric + 2 wrist; hand tracking: IMU (Rokoko Smartgloves); Vive tracker (HTC Vive Tracker); vision (WiLoR, HaMeR); public. arXiv 2605.13083 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13083), [project](https://jianyi2004.github.io/TouchAnything-Website/), [data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/zhouzhoujy/EgoTouch), [code](https://github.com/Jianyi2004/TouchAnything)).
- [TactiDex](https://tactidex.github.io/) - Piezoresistive glove over a MANUS mocap glove with OptiTrack object tracking, single-hand and bimanual, retargeted to Inspire hands. 162 taxels/hand; fingertips, palm; 11.8 h; cameras: none; hand tracking: IMU (MANUS Metagloves Pro); optical mocap (OptiTrack, 8x PrimeX 13W at 120 fps); not released. ACM MM 2026 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09190), [project](https://tactidex.github.io/)).
- [TouchScale](https://touch-scale.github.io/) - Bimanual tactile gloves with 880 taxels per hand, recorded with head-mounted RGB-D and two wrist cameras across about 2K everyday tasks. 880 taxels/hand; fingertips, finger segments, palm; 500 h, 2,000 tasks, 9 environments; cameras: egocentric + 2 wrist; hand tracking: vision (WiLoR); gated. arXiv 2610.10288 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10288), [project](https://touch-scale.github.io/), [data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/2077AIDataFoundation/TouchScale), [code](https://github.com/phai-lab/touchscale)).
- [VICON](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05180) - Glove with eight force-sensing resistors and an IMU/marker hand tracker, recorded with a static RGB-D camera for hand-object pose with calibrated contact force. 8 taxels/hand; fingertips, palm; 0.75 h, 1 tasks; cameras: 1 third-person; hand tracking: IMU (VIST glove); release announced. arXiv 2610.05180 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05180), [code](https://github.com/VICON-dataset/dataset)).
- [OpenTouch](https://opentouch-tactile.github.io/) - In-the-wild right-hand pressure glove with Meta Aria egocentric video, Rokoko hand pose and text labels across 14 environments. 169 taxels/hand; fingertips, finger segments, palm; 5.1 h, 14 environments; cameras: egocentric; hand tracking: IMU (Rokoko Smartgloves); public. arXiv 2512.16842 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16842), [project](https://opentouch-tactile.github.io/), [data](https://github.com/OpenTouch-MIT/opentouch), [code](https://github.com/OpenTouch-MIT/opentouch)).
- [OSMO](https://www.jessicayin.com/osmo_tactile_glove/) - Open-source glove with three-axis magnetic taxels on fingertips and palm; human wiping demonstrations, with the same glove worn by the robot hand at deployment. 12 taxels/hand; fingertips, palm; 2 h, 1 tasks; cameras: 2 third-person; hand tracking: vision (SAM2 mask + HaMeR); vision (HaMeR wrist refined with FoundationStereo depth from the IR pair); joint encoders (Franka arm + Psyonic Ability Hand wearing the OSMO glove); partial. arXiv 2512.08920 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08920), [project](https://www.jessicayin.com/osmo_tactile_glove/), [code](https://github.com/jessicayin/osmo_tactile_glove)).
- [Reach&Grasp](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04552-5) - Upper-limb reaching and grasping with a tactile glove, CyberGlove joint angles, Vicon motion capture and high-density EMG. 58 taxels/hand; fingertips, finger segments, palm; 2.76 h, 16 tasks; cameras: none; hand tracking: optical mocap (Vicon); bend sensors (CyberGlove I); optical mocap (Vicon Plug-in Gait); public. Scientific Data 2025 ([paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04552-5), [code](https://github.com/DarioDiDomenico/SData_ReachGrasp)).
- [VTDexManip](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/e19b6f65791e350347bcff8a3955cb5b-Paper-Conference.pdf) - Piezoresistive tactile gloves with egocentric HoloLens 2 video across 10 manipulation tasks and 182 objects, for visual-tactile pretraining. 20 taxels/hand; fingertips, palm; 10 tasks; cameras: egocentric; hand tracking: none; public. ICLR 2025 ([paper](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/e19b6f65791e350347bcff8a3955cb5b-Paper-Conference.pdf), [data](https://github.com/LQTS/VTDexManip), [code](https://github.com/LQTS/VTDexManip)).
- [ActionSense](https://action-sense.csail.mit.edu/) - Kitchen activities with bimanual tactile gloves, finger bend sensors, EMG, body IMUs, eye tracking and multi-view video. 682 taxels/hand; finger segments, palm; 13 h, 20 tasks; cameras: egocentric + 6 third-person; hand tracking: IMU (Xsens MTw Awinda); bend sensors (Manus Prime II Xsens gloves); public. NeurIPS 2022 Datasets & Benchmarks ([paper](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2022/poster/55638), [project](https://action-sense.csail.mit.edu/), [data](https://action-sense.csail.mit.edu/data.html)).
- [ADL-21](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8068022/) - Tekscan Grip pressure system on the right hand of 22 subjects performing 21 activities of daily living. 361 taxels/hand; fingertips, finger segments, palm; 1.8 h, 21 tasks, 6 environments; cameras: none; hand tracking: none; public. Sensors 2021 ([paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8068022/), [data](https://zenodo.org/records/4607289)).
- [Tactile textiles](https://senstextile.csail.mit.edu/) - Machine-knitted piezoresistive wearables including a full-hand glove; the glove data covers calibration and 26-object classification. 722 taxels/hand; fingertips, finger segments, palm; cameras: none; hand tracking: IMU (Xsens MOCAP); public. Nature Electronics 4 ([paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41928-021-00558-0), [project](https://senstextile.csail.mit.edu/), [data](https://github.com/YunzhuLi/senstextile), [code](https://github.com/YunzhuLi/senstextile)).
- [STAG](http://stag.csail.mit.edu/) - Knitted glove with 548 piezoresistive sensors over the whole hand; 135K frames of single-hand grasps on 26 objects. 548 taxels/hand; fingertips, finger segments, palm; 5 h; cameras: 1 third-person; hand tracking: none; public. Nature 569 ([paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1234-z), [project](http://stag.csail.mit.edu/), [data](http://stag.csail.mit.edu/), [code](https://github.com/Erkil1452/touch)).

## Human-to-robot (paired human and robot touch)

- [DexViTac](https://xitong-c.github.io/DexViTac/) - Human demonstrations with fingertip tactile arrays, Manus finger tracking and a hand-mounted fisheye camera, matched by identical sensors on an Inspire hand. 640 taxels/hand; fingertips; 40 tasks, 10 environments; cameras: 2 wrist; hand tracking: magnetic (Manus Quantum Metagloves); other (Intel RealSense T265); joint encoders (RealMan RM-65 6-DoF arm + Inspire RH56DFTP); release announced. arXiv 2603.17851 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17851), [project](https://xitong-c.github.io/DexViTac/), [data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xitong-c/DexViTac_Dataset)).
- [HRDexDB](https://snuvclab.github.io/HRDexDB/) - Paired human and robot grasps (Allegro, two Inspire hands, Robotiq) with 21 exocentric cameras plus egocentric stereo and object 6D pose; tactile only on the robot hands. taxel count not stated; fingertips, palm; 1 tasks; cameras: 21 third-person + 2 egocentric + 2 other; hand tracking: vision (HaMeR 2D keypoints in 21 views -> triangulation -> MANO fit); joint encoders (robot hand joint states + commands); joint encoders (xArm6 joint states); teleop glove (MANUS gloves); Vive tracker (HTC Vive wrist tracker); public. arXiv 2604.14944 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14944), [project](https://snuvclab.github.io/HRDexDB/), [data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HRDexDB/HRDexDB)).
- [TACROSS](https://tacross-touch-project.github.io/) - Piezoresistive fabric glove for large-scale human touch collection, paired with robot data from a capacitive Revo2 tactile hand. 256 taxels/hand; fingertips, finger segments, palm; 183.95 h; cameras: egocentric + 3 other; hand tracking: vision (HaMeR); IMU (Rokoko motion-capture glove); joint encoders (7-DoF arm); release announced. arXiv 2610.11945 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11945), [project](https://tacross-touch-project.github.io/)).

## Tactile robot hands

- [RoboTacDex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31836) - Visual-tactile-action trajectories on a Unitree G1 with dexterous hands across 19 tasks and 23 skills. taxel count not stated; fingertips; 25 h, 19 tasks; cameras: 1 robot head + 1 third-person + 2 wrist; hand tracking: joint encoders (Unitree G1); vision (Meta Horizon VR headset); release announced. arXiv 2606.31836 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31836)).
- [T-Rex](https://tactile-rex.github.io/) - Bimanual teleoperation on two 22-DoF Sharpa Wave hands with five fingertip tactile sensors each, in LeRobot format. 5 sensors/hand; fingertips; 50 h, 22 tasks, 6 environments; cameras: 1 robot head + 2 wrist; hand tracking: joint encoders (Sharpa Wave 22-DoF hand joint states); joint encoders (Dexmate Vega-1 arm joint encoders); teleop glove (Manus gloves); Vive tracker (HTC VIVE trackers); public. arXiv 2606.17055 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17055), [project](https://tactile-rex.github.io/), [data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/zekaiwang/trex_dataset), [code](https://github.com/ZhuoyangLiu2005/T-Rex)).
- [Humanoid Everyday](https://humanoideveryday.github.io/) - Large humanoid manipulation dataset on Unitree G1 and H1 robots; the G1 episodes use Dex3-1 hands with 9 pressure sensors per hand. 9 sensors/hand; coverage not stated; 31.8 h, 246 tasks; cameras: 1 robot head; hand tracking: joint encoders (Dex3-1); joint encoders (Unitree G1); vision (Apple Vision Pro hand tracking); public. arXiv 2510.08807 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08807), [project](https://humanoideveryday.github.io/), [data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/USC-PSI-Lab/humanoid-everyday)).
- [Humanoid VTA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25725) - Humanoid with two Inspire hands carrying 1,062 tactile sensors each over fingers and palm, teleoperated on deformable-object tasks. 1,062 taxels/hand; fingertips, finger segments, palm; 4 tasks; cameras: 1 robot head + 1 third-person; hand tracking: joint encoders (Inspire RH56DFX finger joint positions); joint encoders (Unitree G1 arm joint positions); vision (Apple Vision Pro hand tracking); not released. arXiv 2510.25725 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25725)).
- [HATO](https://toruowo.github.io/hato/) - Bimanual teleoperation of two Psyonic Ability hands on UR5e arms, with fingertip pressure sensing and three RGB-D cameras. 30 taxels/hand; fingertips; 6.2 h, 4 tasks; cameras: 2 wrist + 1 third-person; hand tracking: joint encoders (PSYONIC Ability Hand finger positions); joint encoders (UR5e joint encoders + EEF pose); other (Meta Quest 2 controllers); other (Meta Quest 2 controller buttons/triggers); public. ICRA 2025 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16823), [project](https://toruowo.github.io/hato/), [data](https://github.com/toruowo/hato), [code](https://github.com/toruowo/hato)).
- [T-Dex](https://tactile-dexterity.github.io/) - Allegro hand covered with XELA uSkin pads, recorded during 2.5 hours of teleoperated play plus task demonstrations. 240 taxels/hand; fingertips, finger segments; 2.5 h, 5 tasks; cameras: 2 third-person; hand tracking: joint encoders (Allegro hand joint states); joint encoders (Kinova Jaco joint + Cartesian states); vision (Holo-Dex); other (Kinova built-in joystick); public. CoRL 2023 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.12076), [project](https://tactile-dexterity.github.io/), [code](https://github.com/irmakguzey/tactile-dexterity)).

## Contributing

Add or correct an entry by editing [`data/datasets.json`](data/datasets.json) and running `python3 scripts/build_readme.py`, or [open an issue](../../issues/new/choose). Please cite a primary source (paper, project page or dataset card) for every number.

## Repository layout

| path | contents |
|---|---|
| `data/datasets.json` | the catalog: one record per dataset, the single source of truth |
| `index.html`, `assets/` | the catalog page (static, no build step) |
| `viewer/` | the synchronized video / tactile / hand-pose player; its media is served from the Hugging Face dataset [`yxma/human-mcap-viewer-media`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yxma/human-mcap-viewer-media) |
| `scripts/build_readme.py` | validates the catalog and regenerates the list above |
| `scripts/fetch_thumbs.py` | caches each dataset's overview figure in `assets/thumbs/` |
